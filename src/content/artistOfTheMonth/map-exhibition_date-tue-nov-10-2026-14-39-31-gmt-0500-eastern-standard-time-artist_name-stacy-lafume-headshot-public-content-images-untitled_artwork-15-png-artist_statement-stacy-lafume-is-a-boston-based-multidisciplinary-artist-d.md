artist_statement: >-
  Stacy Lafume is a Boston-based multidisciplinary artist, digital illustrator,
  and creative technologist whose work explores identity, self-expression, and
  the beauty of human complexity. Through vibrant colors, expressive
  portraiture, and playful experimentation, she challenges conventional
  representations of Blackness, creating space for softness, tenderness, joy,
  and individuality.


  With a background in graphic design and software engineering, Stacy approaches art through a process of exploration, deconstruction, and synthesis. Blending digital illustration, traditional drawing, and influences from pop art and surrealism, she transforms familiar visual elements into imaginative compositions.


  Her work celebrates the contradictions that make us human, inviting viewers to look beyond appearances and embrace the many ways we exist, express ourselves, and connect with one another.
exhibition_statement: >-
  My work explores the beauty of Black identity through softness, individuality,
  and self-expression. I'm drawn to the contrast between how people present
  themselves and how they are perceived. Tattoos, piercings, bold fashion, and
  unconventional appearances are often associated with toughness or rebellion,
  but I see these forms of expression as deeply personal, vulnerable, and human.


  Through portraiture, vibrant colors, and playful compositions, I challenge these assumptions by portraying people with tenderness, warmth, and intimacy. I want to create space for Black people to exist beyond stereotypes, where boldness and softness are not opposing qualities but parts of the same person.


  My creative process is rooted in observation, experimentation, and synthesis. I draw inspiration from familiar imagery, reinterpreting and combining elements to create something that reflects my own perspective.


  Ultimately, my work is an invitation to look beyond appearances and recognize the complexity, beauty, and humanity in one another.
